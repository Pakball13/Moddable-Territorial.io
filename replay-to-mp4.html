How to use

1. In the game (left), click 📜 Replay
2. Paste your replay data and click Launch
3. Set the speed slider here (0.5× – 10×)
4. Click Start Recording
5. Wait — replay runs, frames encode live
6. Click Download MP4 when it stops
The MP4 plays at the speed you choose. 0.5× doubles the recording time; 10× finishes 10× faster.
